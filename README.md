# The-Babio-Ware
Have you ever wanted to play wario ware but with babies? well, probaly not okay

You just won the golden ticket! A free day at the Babio-Ware factory. Experience a day in the life of an employee, doing all the miscellaneous things you could imagine. Keep in mind, you still have to get the work done!!! Failure to complete a task five times results in immediate termination. From the job. Not of you. With a strict manager who needs you to hold his face to clock your work day, it is as easy as you think. Complete a set amount of tasks to earn lunch with the headmaster, but beware, your manager might want to take this prize...

Made in MonoGame
